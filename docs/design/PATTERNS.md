# WAY 2.1 — Proprietary UX Patterns

### 1. `DecisionSurface` Pattern
The primary building block for action-oriented screens across CREED OS.
A `DecisionSurface` replaces raw metric dashboards by prioritizing the user's immediate decision.

#### Anatomy:
1. **Signal:** Visual indicator of state or priority (e.g. Ochre tag *"Today's Focus"*).
2. **Context:** One clear heading and single supporting sentence explaining why this matters.
3. **Action:** Prominent primary CTA button (e.g. *Start 20-Min Practice*).
4. **Evidence:** Compact qualitative badge (e.g. *Based on 2 recent demonstrations*).
5. **Disclosure:** Interactive toggle revealing underlying diagnostic details or rubrics on demand.

---

### 2. `EditorialFeature` Pattern
Used for hero narratives, STEM maker missions, and pathway explorations.

#### Anatomy:
* **Media:** 8px radius fine art mixed-media illustration or authentic workshop capture.
* **Eyebrow:** Semantic domain tag (e.g. *Robotics & Mechatronics*).
* **Title:** Direct invitation into a field (e.g. *Autonomous Rover Kinematics*).
* **Narrative:** Maximum 2 sentences describing what the learner builds and discovers.
* **Action:** Direct interactive trial button (e.g. *Try Hands-on Mission*).

---

### 3. `EvidenceDisclosure` Pattern
Replaces overwhelming evidence lists with clean qualitative summaries and on-demand provenance.

#### Anatomy:
* **Summary State:** Qualitative label (e.g. *"Strong Foundation"*), count of verified atoms (e.g. *"4 verified demonstrations"*), and *"Why? →"* button.
* **Expanded State:** Reveals timestamp, source type (Adaptive Diagnostic, Teacher Observation, Physical Project Artifact), and cryptographic verification badge.

---

### 4. `AIExplainability` Pattern
Adheres to Carbon for AI principles: clearly identifies AI presence without decorative neon, cyan glow, or animated holograms.

#### Anatomy:
* **Badge / Marker:** Subtle iris indicator (*"AI Guided"*).
* **Explainability Trigger:** *"Why am I seeing this?"*
* **Popover Contents:**
  - Evidence used
  - Pedagogical intent (Socratic inquiry, never answer dumping)
  - Boundaries & child-safety constraints
