# WAY 2.1 Foundations

## 1. Philosophical Grounding
WAY 2.1 ("Quiet Editorial Intelligence") is a synthesis of six rigorous design traditions:
1. **Nordic Lagom:** Warm mineral surfaces, intentional whitespace, calm restraint, low visual friction.
2. **IBM Carbon & Carbon Next:** Clear signal hierarchy, density modes, progressive disclosure, actionable workflows.
3. **Apple Human Interface Guidelines:** Tactile clarity, 6-state interactive coverage, responsive typography, natural transitions.
4. **Untitled UI:** Practical SaaS composability, accessible form controls, clean tabular layouts.
5. **Japandi & Editorial Design:** Asymmetric balance (60/40, 70/30), large evocative imagery carrying emotion, minimal text carrying information.
6. **Carbon for AI:** Semantic AI presence, contextual explainability (`Why?`, `Evidence`, `Limitations`), zero decorative glow.

---

## 2. The Three Information Levels Law
Every screen and component in CREED OS strictly adheres to three distinct levels of information:
- **Level 1 — Signal:** What the user must notice immediately. (e.g. Primary recommended action, critical intervention, or foundational strength).
- **Level 2 — Context:** Why it matters. (Expressed in **1 concise sentence maximum**).
- **Level 3 — Evidence:** Verifiable demonstration logs, rubrics, and source atoms. (Disclosed only upon user interaction/click).

---

## 3. The Content Density Rule
To eliminate "information equivalence" (where every metric is boxed and badged):
- **1 Title**
- **1 Short Supporting Sentence Maximum**
- **1 Dominant Primary Action**
- **All deeper diagnostic parameters, calculations, and provenance move into progressive disclosure drawers or dialogs.**
