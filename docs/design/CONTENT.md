# WAY 2.1 Content & Vocabulary Standards

## 1. Role-Aware Vocabulary Strategy

### Student Experience
- **NO:** `Foundation gap`, `Ability estimate`, `Theta`, `SE`, `Mismatch`, `Sub-average`.
- **YES:** `Next focus`, `Demonstrated capability`, `What you're getting good at`, `Skills to build`, `Superpower`.

### Parent Portal
- **NO:** `Standard error`, `Item difficulty`, `Psychometric drift`, `74% career fit`, `Unsuitable`.
- **YES:** `Developing foundation`, `Strong foundation`, `Age-expected`, `How you can help at home`, `Shared horizons`.

### Educator & Guidance Console
- **NO:** Raw ambiguous letter grades or uncontextualized percentiles.
- **YES:** `Actionable misconception cluster`, `Scaffold priority`, `Longitudinal demonstration record`, `3-way dialogue`.

### Psychometric Studio (Console Only)
- **YES:** $\theta$, $SE$, $a/b/c$ 3PL parameters, Fisher Information, EAP ability estimation, item characteristic curves.

---

## 2. Content Density Constraint
- Every UI block has **1 heading**, **1 short sentence**, and **1 action**.
- Never write decorative copy to fill whitespace.
- If a sentence exceeds 140 characters, split it or move background details into an interactive disclosure.
