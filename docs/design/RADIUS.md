# WAY 2.1 Architectural Radius System

## 1. Architectural Philosophy
WAY 2.1 repudiates both rigid zero-radius ideology (which produces harsh, industrial severity) and bubbly consumer curves (which diminish educational authority).
Instead, radii correspond directly to surface functionality.

---

## 2. The Three Radius Tiers

### 0px (`rounded-none`, `--radius-structural: 0px`)
- **Purpose:** Structural, technical, and assessment surfaces.
- **Components:**
  - Active adaptive assessment question frames.
  - Carbon data tables and matrix headers.
  - Console workspace toolbars and terminal views.
  - Raw psychometric information curves.

### 4px (`rounded-sm`, `--radius-control: 4px`)
- **Purpose:** Primary interactive controls and standard content objects.
- **Components:**
  - Buttons (`Button.svelte`).
  - Inputs, selects, and textareas (`Input.svelte`).
  - Standard cards, panels, and tiles (`surface-card`).
  - Modal dialogs, popovers, and drawers.
  - Navigation tabs and pill buttons.

### 8px (`rounded-md`, `--radius-expressive: 8px`)
- **Purpose:** Expressive media, artwork frames, and immersive storytelling.
- **Components:**
  - Hero mixed-media artwork frames (`IllustrationFrame.svelte`).
  - Editorial pathway feature containers (`EditorialFeature.svelte`).
  - High-impact student milestone banners.

---

## 3. Strict Prohibitions
- **FORBIDDEN:** `rounded-lg` (12px), `rounded-xl` (16px), `rounded-2xl` (24px), `rounded-3xl` (32px), pill-shaped action buttons (`rounded-full` on text buttons).
- Fully circular radii (`rounded-full`) are permitted solely for circular avatar chips, progress rings, and miniature status dots.
