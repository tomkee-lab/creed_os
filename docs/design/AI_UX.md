# WAY 2.1 AI User Experience & Governance

## 1. Grounding in Carbon for AI
AI in CREED OS adheres to IBM Carbon for AI foundations:
- **Transparent Identification:** Clearly identify AI-generated content and recommendations.
- **Contextual Explainability:** Explainability appears in context, when useful or requested via progressive disclosure (`AIExplainability`).
- **No Decorative AI Styling:** AI presence is indicated with quiet semantic Iris (`--accent-indigo`), never with purple/cyan glowing borders or animated holograms.

---

## 2. Core Interaction States (Guided Thinking Studio)
The Socratic Guide transitions across five discrete, calm states:
1. **`Listening`:** Pulse indicator on microphone, subtle reactive audio waveform.
2. **`Thinking`:** Subtle primary teal pulse formulating Socratic inquiry.
3. **`Your turn`:** Clear invitational focus prompt for student reasoning.
4. **`Reflecting`:** Guidance toward invariants and mental models.
5. **`Synthesizing`:** Summarizing demonstrated principles and capturing evidence atoms.

---

## 3. The Explainability Triad
Every AI inference or recommendation exposes three explicit layers in its disclosure:
1. **Reasoning Context:** Why the Socratic guide or recommendation engine suggested this action.
2. **Evidence Used:** The specific project trials, diagnostics, or classroom observations backing the insight.
3. **Limitations:** Clear statement that deterministic algorithms score ability while AI solely assists scaffolding.
